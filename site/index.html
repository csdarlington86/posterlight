PosterLight

Now showing at home.

Turn a spare tablet or phone into a theater poster case. It shows the poster and title for whatever is playing on your Plex server.

Open PosterLight See a demo first

Free and open source. Nothing to install. No account with us, no subscription.

The demo runs right in your browser. Its titles and people are made up.

Set it up in about a minute

  1. Open PosterLight on the tablet or phone you want to use: tap Open PosterLight.
  2. Connect it to Plex. The screen shows a short code. On your phone or computer, go to plex.tv/link, sign in, and type the code.
  3. Choose a player to follow, or choose "Any player".
  4. Add it to the home screen for a full-screen poster. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  5. Keep the screen on. On an iPad or iPhone: Settings, Display & Brightness, Auto-Lock, Never.

Then play something in Plex.

What it does

  • A real marquee. Chasing bulbs, a lit poster frame, and an Intermission sign when you pause.
  • Old devices welcome. It runs on an iPad Air 1 with iOS 12, newer iPads and iPhones, Android, and Fire tablets.
  • Easy on the screen. It drifts slowly to prevent burn-in.
  • Every screen in step. Several tablets can follow the same player and show the same thing.

What you need

  • A Plex Media Server, and the Plex account that owns it. Plex shows "what is playing" to the owner only.
  • A tablet or phone. Each screen is connected to Plex once.
  • Plex set up the usual way: secure connections on, which is the default.

Where does my Plex sign-in go? It is saved on your own device and sent only to your own Plex server and to plex.tv. This website has no server behind it and receives nothing: no sign-in, no titles, no viewing history. You sign in on Plex's own page, so PosterLight never sees your password.

Prefer to run it at home?

There is also a small helper program for a computer that stays on. With it, your tablets need no sign-in at all, and it works when a router blocks Plex's secure home addresses.
